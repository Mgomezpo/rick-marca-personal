# 📚 Libro Canónico: The Creative Act: A Way of Being
**Autor:** Rick Rubin  
**Dominio Epistémico:** `Creativity, Art, and Consciousness`  
**Estatus:** Fuente Primaria de Verdad (Axiomática)

---

## Tesis Central y Marco Epistémico
To create is not a rare ability for a chosen few, but a fundamental aspect of being human. Living as an artist is a way of being in the world: a continuous practice of paying attention, tuning in to the subtle notes of the universe, cultivating openness, and allowing the creative impulse to channel through without attachment to outcome.
